01

The question

Fast in a test.
Useful in a game?

Our candidate is sustained speed in the receiver Gauntlet: a football drill that offers repeated attempts to examine how consistently a player moves.

A new speed ranking is only a starting point. We first need an interpretable measurement, agreement between attempts, and comparable movement opportunities in NFL routes.

The eventual comparison asks whether that measurement improves prediction for new prospects beyond conventional Combine results. A positive result is a question to test, not an assumption.

The method

Test the transfer.
Protect the future.

Proposed evaluation, gated on a defensible measurement. Conventional Combine tests and the added feature will be compared on the same players.

  1. 01 / Current

    2023

    Develop

    Define windows, inspect trajectories, quantify repeatability, and choose one feature and endpoint.

  2. 02 / Planned

    2024

    Validate

    Apply the frozen protocol to a later cohort. Report the result and uncertainty.

  3. 03 / Planned

    2023–24

    Refit

    Refit the predeclared baseline and augmented model on the available development cohorts.

  4. 04 / Planned

    2025

    Final test

    Save predictions before joining final outcomes. Compare held-out errors and retain null results.

This measurement checkpoint used 2023 physical trajectories and metadata. Numerical NFL performance outcomes and 2024/2025 game kinematics remain outside this development work.

04

Next steps

The next four decisions.

Study viability remains pending. The next work answers whether Gauntlet speed is measurable and comparable before testing its predictive value.

  1. 01

    Define the window.

    Inspect Gauntlet geometry and whole-drill speed curves; specify one reproducible sustained-speed window.

  2. 02

    Test the repeats.

    Quantify agreement between attempts and sensitivity to timing choices at the observed sampling resolution.

  3. 03

    Match the demand.

    Audit comparable NFL route windows and acceleration opportunities. A shared player ID alone does not establish comparable movement.

  4. 04

    Register the comparison.

    Freeze one feature, one endpoint, the conventional baseline, and the evaluation rules after the measurement gates pass.
